## Design Rationale (R-31 Reference)

- **Color (`#F4F3EF` canvas, `#E8E5DE` surface, `#141413` ink, `#1B5564` mineral teal accent):** Warm archival stone avoids sterile `#FFFFFF` and generic `#F4F1EA` cream+terracotta defaults while keeping all text pairs at `6.00:1` to `16.60:1` contrast.
- **Typography (`Host Grotesk`, `Newsreader`, `IBM Plex Mono`):** `Host Grotesk` provides crisp European neo-grotesk proportions outside AI default/trend lists; `Newsreader` adds editorial contrast for publishing specimens; `IBM Plex Mono` is restricted to `12–13px` index/hex metadata.
- **Layout (`7/5`, `5/7`, `12` asymmetric grid):** Mirrors the visual-first editorial pacing of `studioblue.agency` and `several.club` while keeping mobile (`390px`) single-column with zero horizontal overflow.
- **Motion (`GSAP` + `<ClientRouter />`):** Subtle `14–18px` entrance/reveal transforms and `1.025x` image hover scale guide attention without hiding initial paint (`opacity: 1`) and disable cleanly under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

## Architectural Cylinders

Thrown straight-walled and collared slightly at the rim, each cylinder features a hand-pinched pouring lip that cuts cleanly without dripping. Sold as a graduated trio or individual table carafe.

## Glaze Chemistry

Dipped in a high-feldspar chalk glaze over iron-bearing stoneware, the surface develops subtle warm bands where the glaze pools along horizontal turning lines.

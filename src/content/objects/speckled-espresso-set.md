## Daily Ergonomics

Each cup is thrown with a slightly tapered lip for clean pouring and a hand-pulled strap handle sized for two fingers. The 4 mm wall thickness keeps espresso warm without burning the hand.

## Batch Variation

Iron particles suspended in the clay body bloom through the white tin glaze during reduction firing, giving every pair a distinct constellation of dark mineral flecks.

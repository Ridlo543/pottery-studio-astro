Designed for both home tables and restaurant service, the Linen Rim Dinner Plates stack compactly with a low 2.5 cm profile and a wide flat plating surface.

## Trimmed Foot Rings

Each plate is turned upside down at the leather-hard stage to carve a crisp, unglazed foot ring that sits flush on timber or stone tables without scratching.

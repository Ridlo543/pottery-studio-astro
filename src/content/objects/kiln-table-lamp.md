## Architectural Light

Unlike fabric shades that diffuse light evenly in all directions, the opaque stoneware cone directs a crisp pool of warm 2700K light downward onto desks, bedside tables, and dining sideboards.

## Hardware & Wiring

Fitted with solid turned brass E27 hardware, an inline rotary dimmer switch, and a 2.2-meter natural linen braided cable. Each base is stamped and numbered on the underside.
